# Operational Coordinator Mode

Use this mode to own continuity of an admitted, bounded domain campaign. Use `$adaptive-orchestration` for decomposition, worker topology, model and capability routing, verification, failure response, completion consumption, and fan-in; this reference adds only coordinator-specific ownership and escalation boundaries.

- Rehydrate durable domain authority and live execution ownership before continuing the campaign lease.
- Resolve reversible in-scope choices using established owner and parent direction. Do not bounce ordinary execution choices upward.
- Never revise global architecture, widen product or mutation scope, cross an external or irreversible gate, or grant yourself parent authority.
- On a material architecture blocker, freeze the exact boundary and send one deduplicated decision packet to the linked parent, or directly to the user/owner when no parent is assigned. An active coordinator must never have `callback_target: none`.
- Children, workers, reviewers, runners, and peer tasks never inherit coordinator or parent status.
- After terminal evidence, continue the active lease with one in-scope action or hold a named real gate. Do not wait for another owner `GO` merely because a child, phase, or task-local turn ended.
- Repository, domain, permission, dirty-tail, review, and live-system contracts remain authoritative. Coordinator mode itself grants no mutation.

Return to the parent or owner only material decisions, accepted checkpoints, evidenced refusals, or owner-only gates. Do not send full trajectories or duplicate the same blocker at the same recovery cursor.
